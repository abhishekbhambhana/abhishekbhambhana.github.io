01Upwork growth

Upwork Growth Desk

Most of my week goes here. Upwork Growth Desk runs Upwork accounts for developers, agencies and designers who would rather do the work than chase it.

We choose which jobs to bid on, write each proposal for the client who will read it, take the interviews and follow ups, and keep a close eye on Job Success so one bad contract doesn't undo a year of good ones. Every account gets a pipeline report each week.

It's a flat monthly fee with no commission on what you win. If an account has no real traction by month three, months four and five are free.

Some teams would rather run it themselves. For them I do profile audits, Upwork SEO, title and overview rewrites, portfolio plans, and a proposal framework their bidders can follow.

Accounts I manage today: MERN, AI and Gen AI, DevOps and AWS, Laravel, WordPress, Shopify, Salesforce, Java and Spring, Flutter, React and Next.js, SaaS, brand and design, Meta Ads, iOS, Odoo.

How an account wins

  1. 01Score the jobClient history, budget and fit, checked before a Connect is spent.
  2. 02Write to one clientThe first two lines answer their post. Nothing copied from the last one.
  3. 03InterviewCalls and follow ups, handled for you.
  4. 04CloseOffer, scope and milestones agreed before anyone starts.
  5. 05Protect Job SuccessEvery contract watched, so one bad review doesn't cost a year.
02Go to market

Go to market, from zero

From December 2024 to March 2025 I worked as a fractional head of sales for five early startups. Each one needed the same first things: a clear picture of who buys, a cold email and LinkedIn routine someone could actually keep up, and a discovery call that ends with a next step.

Across the five, leads went up by about half and sales cycles got 30 to 45% shorter. One of them grew revenue 5x.

UnitPay

Reporting to founder Vijay Gorfad

At UnitPay I worked on growth and sales end to end. I decided which channels deserved our time, tightened the sales cycle from first reply to signed contract, and worked on the investor side: the pitch story, the decks, and the conversations around them.

Upwork was the main channel. In Vijay's words, the account grew past $100K in revenue.
